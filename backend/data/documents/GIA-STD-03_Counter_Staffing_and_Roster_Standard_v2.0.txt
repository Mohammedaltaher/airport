This is a fictional document created for training purposes.

Section 1. Purpose
1.1 This standard sets how many counters, desks and lanes must be staffed at each checkpoint, and how rosters are built and changed.
1.2 It supports the service targets in the Passenger Service Standards Policy (GIA-POL-01).

Section 2. Roster templates and minimum counters
2.1 Three roster templates are used. Standard applies Monday to Thursday, Friday-B applies on Fridays, Weekend applies on Saturday and Sunday.
2.2 Departure immigration, Terminal 1: minimum 8 counters off-peak and 9 counters in peak hours (17:00 to 22:59). 14 counters are installed.
2.3 Departure immigration, Terminal 2: minimum 5 counters off-peak and 5 counters in peak hours. 9 counters are installed.
2.4 Arrival immigration: minimum 6 counters in Terminal 1 and 3 counters in Terminal 2 at all times except 00:00 to 04:59.
2.5 Check-in desks: Terminal 1 minimum 28 off-peak and 36 in peak hours; Terminal 2 minimum 18 off-peak and 24 in peak hours.
2.6 Security lanes: Terminal 1 minimum 5 off-peak and 7 in peak hours; Terminal 2 minimum 3 off-peak and 4 in peak hours.

Section 3. Night hours
3.1 From 00:00 to 04:59 the minimum departure immigration counters are 4 in Terminal 1 and 2 in Terminal 2.
3.2 From 05:00 to 06:59 the minimum is 7 in Terminal 1 and 4 in Terminal 2.

Section 4. Absences and shortfalls
4.1 A reserve pool covers short-notice absence. A shortfall of more than one counter against the template must be reported to the duty manager.
4.2 Counters left closed through absence are not an exception under section 5.

Section 5. Exceptions
5.1 An exception to a minimum in section 2 needs the written approval of the Director, Airport Operations, must have an expiry date and must be listed in this section.
5.2 Active exception EX-2025-07: the Friday-B template reduces Terminal 1 departure immigration to 5 counters between 17:00 and 22:59 on Fridays. It was approved on 1 October 2025 pending a Friday demand study, on the assumption that Friday evening demand is lower than other evenings. It is valid until 30 June 2026.
5.3 No renewal of exception EX-2025-07 is recorded. After 30 June 2026 the Friday-B template must provide the Standard peak minimum of 9 counters.
5.4 The Friday demand study (reference DS-2025-11) was due in March 2026 and has not been completed.

Section 6. Roster change control
6.1 A change to a roster template needs a review of the previous 3 months of waiting-time data for the affected checkpoint.
6.2 Rosters are reviewed monthly under section 3.4 of GIA-POL-01.
6.3 This standard is reviewed annually. The next review is due on 1 October 2026.
