This is a fictional document created for training purposes.

Section 1. Status of this draft
1.1 This document is a consultation draft. It is NOT approved and has NO effect. The rules in force are in GIA-POL-01 v3.0.
1.2 Comments are due by 30 November 2026 to the Director, Passenger Experience.
1.3 Every number in this draft is a proposal and may change.

Section 2. Proposed changes to processing time targets
2.1 Proposed: departure immigration average wait not to exceed 12 minutes (currently 15), and no more than 3 percent of departing passengers waiting longer than 30 minutes in any peak hour (currently 5 percent).
2.2 Proposed: check-in and security averages not to exceed 8 minutes (currently 10).
2.3 Proposed: arrival immigration average not to exceed 12 minutes (currently 15).

Section 3. Proposed changes to peak hours and counters
3.1 Proposed: on Fridays, peak hours start at 16:00 instead of 17:00.
3.2 Proposed: minimum departure immigration counters in peak hours become 10 in Terminal 1 (currently 9) and 6 in Terminal 2 (currently 5).
3.3 Proposed: no exception to a staffing minimum may last longer than 90 days.
3.4 Proposed: smart gate users are reported separately from staffed counter users.

Section 4. Proposed changes to satisfaction and missed flights
4.1 Proposed: average satisfaction of at least 4.2 in every category (currently 4.0).
4.2 Proposed: an executive review is triggered when more than 0.5 percent of departing passengers miss their flight in any peak hour band (currently 1 percent).
4.3 Proposed pilot: a goodwill voucher of up to AED 150 for passengers who miss a flight because of an airport queue longer than 45 minutes. This would change section 4.1 of GIA-SOP-05, which today excludes cash compensation.

Section 5. Consultation and timetable
5.1 Consultation closes on 30 November 2026. The revised draft goes to the Executive Committee in December 2026.
5.2 If approved, the policy takes effect on 1 January 2027. Until then GIA-POL-01 v3.0 applies.
