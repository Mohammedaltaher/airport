Section 1. Status of this document
1.1 This version was replaced by GIA-POL-01 v3.0 on 1 January 2026. It is kept for audit and for assessing performance during its period of validity, 1 April 2024 to 31 December 2025.
1.2 It must not be used to judge performance after 31 December 2025.

Section 2. Processing time targets (v2.0)
2.1 Departure check-in: the average wait must not exceed 12 minutes.
2.2 Departure security screening: the average wait must not exceed 12 minutes.
2.3 Departure immigration: the average wait must not exceed 20 minutes, and no more than 10 percent of departing passengers may wait longer than 30 minutes in any peak hour.
2.4 Arrival immigration: the average wait must not exceed 20 minutes.

Section 3. Peak hours and counters (v2.0)
3.1 Peak departure hours are 18:00 to 22:59 every day of the week.
3.2 During peak departure hours a minimum of 8 departure immigration counters must be open in Terminal 1 and 5 in Terminal 2.
3.3 If the average departure immigration wait exceeds 40 minutes for 3 consecutive hours, the duty manager must request additional border staff.

Section 4. Satisfaction and missed flights (v2.0)
4.1 The average satisfaction score must be at least 3.8 out of 5 in every survey category.
4.2 An executive review is mandatory if more than 2 percent of departing passengers miss their flight in any peak hour band.
