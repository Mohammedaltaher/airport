This is a fictional document created for training purposes.

Section 1. Purpose
1.1 This policy sets how the airport earns revenue from shops, food and beverage, car rental and services, and how outlet performance is managed.

Section 2. Rent and revenue share
2.1 Concession rent is a share of sales: 22 percent for duty free, 18 percent for food and beverage, 15 percent for other retail and 12 percent for car rental.
2.2 An outlet may be on fixed rent instead of revenue share if approved by the Commercial Committee.

Section 3. Revenue targets
3.1 Average spend per departing passenger in shops, food and beverage must be at least AED 50.
3.2 Non-aeronautical revenue per passenger must be at least AED 32.
3.3 Passengers who spend longer at the airport after processing spend more. The Commercial team must be informed when processing delays exceed the service standards, because they reduce spend.

Section 4. Outlet performance
4.1 An outlet whose monthly sales are below AED 1,000 per square metre for 6 consecutive months is placed under performance review.
4.2 A performance review may lead to a new layout, a new offer, a rent change or non-renewal of the lease.
4.3 Sales per square metre are calculated from monthly sales divided by the outlet floor area.

Section 5. Reporting
5.1 Commercial reports monthly sales, rent and transactions by outlet, and compares outlet totals with daily revenue reporting.
