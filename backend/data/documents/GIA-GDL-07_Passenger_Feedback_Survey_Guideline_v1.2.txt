This is a fictional document created for training purposes.

Section 1. Purpose
1.1 This guideline explains how passenger feedback is collected, scored and reported, so that results are comparable month to month.

Section 2. Collection
2.1 Passengers are invited to rate one category after their journey through four channels: Kiosk, QR code, Mobile app and SMS.
2.2 Roughly one passenger in eight responds. Each response is linked to the passenger journey.
2.3 Responses may be written in English or Arabic. Both languages are analysed together.

Section 3. Scoring and categories
3.1 Scores run from 1 (very poor) to 5 (excellent).
3.2 Categories are Waiting time, Staff, Cleanliness, Shops and dining, Wi-Fi, Signage, Security, Baggage, and Parking and transport.
3.3 A score of 1 or 2 is negative, 3 is neutral, 4 or 5 is positive.

Section 4. Data quality
4.1 Exact duplicate submissions (same journey, category, score and comment) must be removed before any result is reported.
4.2 A category is reported only when there are at least 30 valid responses.

Section 5. Using comments
5.1 Free-text comments are read for themes. Themes found in negative comments are compared with the operational data for the same terminal and time.
5.2 A recurring theme in negative comments, in the same time slot, is raised as a systemic issue under GIA-SOP-05.

Section 6. Reporting
6.1 Monthly results are reported by terminal and category against the targets in GIA-POL-01 section 5.
